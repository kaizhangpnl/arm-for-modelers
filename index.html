ARM For Modelers

As part of the DOE ARM User Executive Committee (UEC) Subgroup on observation/modeling integration, we are organizing a series of online mini-workshops and seminars focused on strengthening the integration of ARM observations with atmospheric modeling research. The series will be held approximately every two months, beginning in fall 2026, with the goal of encouraging sustained dialogue between the observational and modeling communities. Planned topics and activities include:

  • Data applications: new opportunities, best practices, and common pitfalls. ARM data experts will introduce newly available datasets, highlight practical use cases, share recommended workflows, and discuss lessons learned from common data-application challenges.
  • Identifying and communicating model successes and limitations. Modelers will highlight key modeling challenges and limitations to help observational experts better understand process-representation gaps, evaluation needs, and priority areas for further research.
  • Sharing new software and diagnostic tools. Participants will introduce emerging software, analysis frameworks, and diagnostic tools that can support the use of ARM observations in model development and evaluation.
